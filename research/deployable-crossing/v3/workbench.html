Keep the counterfactual explicit

H₀ − H₁ = N₀r₀ − N₁[au·rᵦ + (1 − au)r𝒻] − D − S

N₀ and N₁ are person-crossings. Availability a is weighted by crossing demand; uptake u is conditional on accessible availability. Baseline, facility and fallback risks remain separate. D and S cover nonoverlapping deployment and displacement harm.

The reference bridge does not supply measured local risk, demand or benefit. Input ranges describe scenarios; they are not confidence intervals or a lives-saved forecast.
