# Crest–Root research register

**23 November 2026.** The original register had 57 entries, including engineering, publication and two dormant projects. Its priority counts were 8 at P0, 25 at P1, 22 at P2 and 2 at P3. The reconciled workbook records progress through Part XXIII, §102. The mathematical edition now reaches Part XXX, §148. This crosswalk retains all 57 original IDs and priorities. [Original register][original] · [Part XXIII workbook][workbook] · [Part XXX release][xxx]

The largest change concerns degree-three maps from smooth plane quartics over the rationals to genus-one curves. Theorem 138.1 gives a terminating procedure for the **geometric quotient count**, up to target isomorphism. It takes the quartic equation as input. The second major closure is Theorem 100.1, which proves the cubic tower construction for every finite stage in characteristic zero. General local-to-global correspondence discovery, practical runtime on arbitrary quartics and rational-point determination on descended genus-one targets remain separate questions. [Part XXIII, §100][xxiii] · [Part XXVIII, §§125–133][xxviii] · [Part XXX, §§138–148][xxx]

The last undecided row in the twelve-curve blind sample, Sutherland 23640, now has a degree-two quotient and zero degree-three quotients. A single good reduction proves the same all-degree classification for infinitely many geometrically distinct quartics. This advances G06, F04 and F12. [Proof and executable certificate][new]

“Carried forward” means the cited register listed the question as open and the subsequent materials reviewed here supplied no closure. “Partial” identifies a later result relevant to the original request. “Settled” identifies the exact scope of a theorem or a completed delivery. The review covers this project’s records. A claim of mathematical novelty or an external literature search would require separate evidence.
