# Primary sources and scope

These sources support the feasibility discussion. They do not validate the new program, establish capacity of a proposed machine, or replace project-specific engineering. Dates refer to the source's publication where given; the sources were consulted on 23 November 2026.

| Source | Specific use in this study | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [CN106012799A, Pedestrian overbridge vehicle, 2016](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN106012799A/en) | Opposed vehicles, support legs, elevated extending halves, and meeting-end connectors; Figure 6. | A disclosure, not verified field performance or a legal-status opinion. |
| [CN106012799A original PDF](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/a3/c8/6d/99976608377b54/CN106012799A.pdf) | Original drawings and Chinese text, especially Figure 6. | The English page is machine translated; specific implementation terms require care. |
| [US12480266B1, Rapid deploy mobile pedestrian bridge, 2025](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12480266B1/en) | Truck-mounted lifting and one-span scissor extension to a receiving truck. | Timing and capacity assertions are not adopted as test evidence. |
| [Firth and Bahra, Copenhagen's 'kissing' bridge, Ingenia, 2017](https://ingenia.org.uk/articles/copenhagens-kissing-bridge/) | First-hand description of a built two-cantilever sliding bridge and changing reactions. | Permanent foundations and supports differ substantially from mobile roadside units. |
| [APS, Grand Prix Overpass 1](https://apsystems.net.au/project-examples/grand-prix-overpass-1-temporary-modular-overpass/) | Contractor's account of a delivered modular temporary pedestrian overpass. | Uses conventional assembly and cranage; not a two-half self-deploying machine. |
| [U.S. Access Board, Ramps and Curb Ramps](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-4-ramps-and-curb-ramps/) | Ramp slope, run rise and landing geometry used in the illustrative footprint calculation. | A geometry example does not determine the governing requirements for a particular public road. |
| [U.S. Access Board, Public Right-of-Way Accessibility Guidelines](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/complete.html) | R203.7 overpasses and suitable elevators; R407 ramps; adoption-dependent applicability. | Agency adoption and project applicability must be established. |
| [TxDOT Roadway Design Manual, Vertical Clearance](https://www.txdot.gov/content/txdotoms/us/en/manuals/des/rdw/chapter-4--basic-design-criteria/4-8-vertical-alignment/4-8-6-vertical-clearance.html) | Example of an agency-specific clearance policy that varies by network. | Texas-specific; no clearance in this study is selected from it as a universal value. |
| [FHWA, Manual on Use of SPMTs, Chapter 4](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/pubs/07022/chap04.cfm) | Temporary supports, ground bearing, moving-state forces, deformation and wind. | Guidance for bridge movement; not a complete specification for this device. |
| [FHWA, Engineering for Structural Stability in Bridge Construction](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/pubs/nhi15044.pdf) | Strength and stability during erection stages, bracing, load reversals and fit-up. | The proposed mechanism and load cases still require their own model. |
| [WSDOT Terminal Design Manual, Chapter 620](https://www.wsdot.wa.gov/publications/manuals/fulltext/M3082/620.pdf) | An official application-specific example addressing pedestrian loads and walkway vibration. | Passenger-terminal requirements are not asserted as the complete governing code for this project. |
| [FHWA MUTCD, 11th Edition with Revision 1](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/kno_11th_Editionr1.htm) | Current traffic-control reference for planning work affecting road users. | A road authority must develop or approve the actual deployment traffic plan. |
| [FHWA, Pedestrian Safety Guide for Transit Agencies, Chapter 3](https://highways.dot.gov/safety/pedestrian-bicyclist/pedestrian-safety-guide-transit-agencies/chapter-3-actions-increase) | Convenience, approach detours and likelihood of crossing use. | General planning guidance, not a measured safety benefit for this concept. |

The support equations, interval calculations and rank examples are elementary mechanics and linear algebra derived in `proof.md`. They are not attributed to the patent authors and are not presented as new theorems. A future novelty or commercialization review would be a separate task; this study makes no freedom-to-operate determination.
