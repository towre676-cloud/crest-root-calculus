Can you hand out 200 different puzzles?

Every lantern is dark or lit in one of the available colors. Under the alternating rule, dark and lit take turns. The separated rule also permits neighboring darks. Change the rule, then pick a numbered strip to hand to a player.

Which points land together?

Choose a point on the simpler curve v² = u³ − 29u − 59. The three points that land there on the quartic C⋆ form its fibre. These three worked targets use the project’s recovered map.

Check a point in the forward direction

Enter whole numbers or fractions for the three coordinates. Multiplying all three by the same nonzero number describes the same point.
