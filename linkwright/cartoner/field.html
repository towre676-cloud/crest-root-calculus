Mechanism geometry

What the four-bar proves

The published pivot distances are 95 mm for ground, 30 mm for the crank, 104 mm for the coupler and 60 mm for the rocker. They leave 21 mm folded and 39 mm extended nominal closure margin over a full turn. Carton clearance needs its own measurement or geometry.

Direct carton-handling evidence

When a suction cup detaches

Calabrese and colleagues recorded pressure at 10 kHz on four cups moving a carton through a 134 ms cycle. Their three experiments show normal operation, one cup detached and two cups detached. The authors report loss of function with two detached cups.
