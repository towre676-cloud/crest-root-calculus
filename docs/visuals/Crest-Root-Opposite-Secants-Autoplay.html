Opposite secants over the field with three elements

A, B, C and D are the four points on a conic over the field with three elements. A–C and B–D are opposite secants. Their shared point P is the only internal point on each. At degrees prime to 3, all contributing vectors reduce to internal points, so both secants count the same vectors. In the displayed example degree 9 is the first difference. Four primitive vectors reduce to A or C and none lie on B–D.
